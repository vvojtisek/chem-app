# Curriculum sources and credits

This application is an independent learning aid. A source attribution does not
mean that VŠCHT created, reviewed, endorsed, or operates the application.

## Preparation and production

The product-indexed preparation and manufacturing material is derived from the
[VŠCHT e-learning equation index](https://e-learning.vscht.cz/echo/anorganika/PripravyVyroby/indexes/equationsIndexByProduct.html).
The structured records preserve source equation IDs, formulas, conditions, and
separate notes. Automated validation checks formula parsing, atom balance, and
coefficient ratios. This does not establish chemical plausibility. The 116
enabled equations are owner-approved and are awaiting chemistry-SME review; one
unbalanced source equation is withheld from learning and practice.

## Inorganic nomenclature

The published practice set includes entries derived from the
[VŠCHT e-learning nomenclature materials](https://e-learning.vscht.cz/mod/page/view.php?id=13158&lang=cs).
The 469 enabled entries are owner-approved, not chemistry-SME reviewed. Their
source, status, aliases, and review questions are recorded in
`content/data/nomenclature.json` and the review ledgers under
`docs/exec-plans/active/`.

## Elements and memory aids

The 118 element records have a current chemistry-SME review. Group names and
their original memory aids remain a separate content review: none of the eight
named groups has a current chemistry-SME approval. Optional alternative
mnemonics were supplied by the content owner and are labeled as such in the
learning material; they are not represented as reviewed scientific content.

Record-level provenance is maintained in `content/data/`. User-created or
locally edited cards are learner notes, not part of the reviewed curriculum.
